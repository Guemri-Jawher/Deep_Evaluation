# Judge checklist

Questions to answer when someone proposes an LLM judge. Retrieve the guide's wording first.

1. Which mode: direct scoring or pairwise comparison?
2. Is the rubric concrete per score level, reasoning-before-score, and structured output?
3. Which biases are controlled: position, verbosity, self-preference, leniency, format?
4. Is the judge validated against human labels (agreement metric such as Cohen's kappa)?
5. What does it cost, and can a cheaper model reach the same agreement?
